🎓 KEY INSIGHTS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. SMALLER IS BETTER (for quantum with noise):
   ✓ 4 qubits outperforms 6-8 qubits
   ✓ 2 layers outperforms 3-5 layers
   → Reason: NISQ hardware noise dominates larger circuits

2. REGULARIZATION IS CRITICAL:
   ✓ L2=0.01 significantly better than L2=0.005
   ✓ Prevents overfitting to quantum noise
   → Reason: Quantum gradients are inherently noisy

3. BATCH SIZE MATTERS:
   ✓ Larger batches (32) more stable than smaller (8,16)
   → Reason: Averages out noisy quantum measurements

4. AGGRESSIVE DECAY HELPS:
   ✓ Decay rate 0.90 better than 0.95
   → Reason: Finds better local optima faster

5. MODERATE LEARNING RATE:
   ✓ 0.02 sweet spot (not too high/low)
   → Reason: Balance between speed and stability
